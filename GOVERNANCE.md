# Governance

## Current Model: Benevolent Dictator

SOVA is maintained by SecureNexus. All final decisions on direction,
architecture, and releases are made by the maintainers listed in
MAINTAINERS.md.

## Contributions

All contributions are welcome via pull request. See CONTRIBUTING.md
for the process.

## Future

As the contributor community grows, we intend to evolve toward a
meritocratic governance model with multiple committers and a
technical steering committee. This document will be updated when
that transition occurs.
